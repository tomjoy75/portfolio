The fractals above are rendered by the same C that drives the native MiniLibX build.

Seven source files compile to WebAssembly unchanged — the maths, the colour palettes, the zoom and pan arithmetic and the key handlers. Only the MiniLibX application layer is replaced, by around 150 lines of glue. Selecting a palette or nudging the Julia constant still executes the original handlers in `srcs/keys.c`; none of those constants are transcribed into JavaScript.
